## Component Details

### 1. Temporal Buffer

*Responsibility:*
Store the most recent frames before sending them to the sequence model.

*Input:*
63-dimensional feature vector from each frame.

*Output:*
A sequence of feature vectors (e.g., 30 consecutive frames).

---

### 2. Sequence Generator

*Responsibility:*
Convert buffered landmark vectors into a fixed-length sequence suitable for deep learning models.

*Input:*
Buffered landmark vectors.

*Output:*
A sequence matrix (e.g., 30 × 63).

---

### 3. Sequence Model (LSTM / GRU / Transformer)

*Responsibility:*
Learn temporal relationships between consecutive frames to recognize dynamic gestures.

*Input:*
Sequence matrix generated by the Sequence Generator.

*Output:*
Predicted gesture or word with confidence score.

---

### 4. Gesture / Word Prediction

*Responsibility:*
Convert the model output into the recognized sign or word.

*Input:*
Prediction probabilities from the sequence model.

*Output:*
Final recognized gesture or word.

---

### 5. Sentence Formation

*Responsibility:*
Combine multiple predicted words into meaningful sentences.

*Input:*
Sequence of predicted words.

*Output:*
Complete sentence displayed to the user.
